CONCLUSION
==========

Merge Sort has predictable O(n log n) worst-case time complexity
and requires O(n) additional space.

Quick Sort generally requires less auxiliary array space, but
its worst-case time complexity can become O(n^2) depending on
pivot selection.

For large datasets where predictable worst-case performance is
important, Merge Sort can be justified as the suitable approach.
